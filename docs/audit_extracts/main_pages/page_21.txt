The descriptive analytics results are interpreted by
examining the magnitude, trend, and differences of
the calculated measures across reporting periods and
branches. In sales analysis, an increase in the
calculated sales value across reporting periods
indicates increased recorded sales activity, while
differences among branches identify variations in
sales performance. In collection analysis, differences
in total collections or collection rates indicate changes
in recorded collection activity. Inventory analysis
identifies differences in available stock and inventory
movement, while customer payment and credit
analysis identifies the concentration of outstanding
balances and delinquent accounts. These interpreted
results provide management with evidence for
determining which areas require further review or
action. For example, a sustained decrease in a
branch’s sales or collection measures may prompt
management to review the corresponding activities.
