2.3.2.9. Generation of Priority List and Route
After computing the preference scores, CORVEX
arranges customers from highest to lowest priority
score. The resulting ranked customer list becomes the
daily collection schedule presented in the Collector's
Field Portal. Based on the generated priority list, the
system retrieves the geographic coordinates of the
prioritized customers and submits them to the routing
service to determine an efficient travel sequence. The
generated priority list, together with the
recommended route, enables collectors to perform
daily collection activities using a standardized,
data-driven schedule instead of relying on subjective
judgment.
