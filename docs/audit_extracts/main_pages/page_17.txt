2.3.2.7. Computation of Weighted Preference
Scores
After normalization, CORVEX calculates each
customer's preference score by multiplying each
normalized criterion value by its corresponding
weight and summing the results. This computation
determines the overall priority score the Collection
Dispatch Engine uses to rank customer accounts.
